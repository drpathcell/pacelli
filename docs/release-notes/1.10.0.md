Two different things stop being one button.

• "Burn all data" has become "Delete data", with the two things it used to do kept apart. Burning the household's shared content — tasks, checklists, plans, photos, the manual — is now separate from deleting your own account.

• The person who created the household chooses who can erase what everyone shares: only them, only people they name, anyone in the household, or nobody. Households that already exist start at "only the creator", which is a change from anyone being able to.

• Deleting your own account is always available to everyone and is never affected by that setting.

• Long task titles now wrap instead of being cut off when you open a task.
