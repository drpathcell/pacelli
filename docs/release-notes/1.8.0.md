Photos, on anything.

• Add a photo to a task, a subtask or a shopping item. Take one or pick one — it appears straight away, and everyone in the household sees it.

• A shopping item shows its picture in the list, so whoever does the shop gets the right brand without opening anything.

• All your photos in one place — Search now has a Photos view. Every picture with what it belongs to underneath.

• Find a photo by typing. Your phone reads the words in a picture, so a boiler serial number or the name on a coffee bag becomes something you can search for. That happens on your phone; no photo is sent anywhere to be looked at.

• The readable copy of every photo stays on your own phone, in a Pacelli folder you can open in the Files app. What is stored for the household is encrypted, like everything else. Location data is removed before a photo is saved.

• Your data export now includes your photos.
