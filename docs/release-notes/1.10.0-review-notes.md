No account is required — tap "Continue as guest" on the welcome screen and the
app is fully usable.

This release splits one destructive action into two, and adds a household
setting that governs ONE of them.

To test:

1. The split. Settings -> Danger zone -> "Delete data". The screen now has two
   sections. "Household data" erases the content the household shares and
   leaves every account and membership intact. "Your account" deletes the
   signed-in account, its profile, its encryption keys and its membership. On a
   guest account both are reachable and both work.

2. Account deletion (Guideline 5.1.1(v)). Settings -> Danger zone -> "Delete
   data" -> "Delete my account" -> confirm. Returns to the welcome screen with
   the account gone. This path is available on every account type including
   guest, is never hidden, and is not affected by the setting in point 3 —
   see the note below.

3. The household setting. Settings -> Danger zone -> "Delete data" -> "Who can
   burn this data" (shown only to the account that created the household) ->
   choose "Nobody" -> Save. The "Burn household data" button disappears and is
   replaced by a line explaining why. "Delete my account" is still there and
   still works. Choosing "Anyone in the household" brings the button back.

Notes for review:

- **Account deletion is not gated, and cannot be.** The setting added in this
  release applies only to erasing content the household SHARES with other
  people. Deleting your own account is available to every user on every account
  type, at all times, and no household member — including the household's
  creator — can restrict it. This is enforced in two places: the account
  deletion path never consults the setting, and the server function that
  performs a household burn is a separate endpoint that account deletion does
  not call.

- Why the setting exists: any member of a household could previously erase
  everything every other member had put into it. Since the previous release a
  household member can also be a connected AI assistant, which made "any member
  may wipe everything" a wider statement than anyone had agreed to.

- When the person deleting their account is the LAST member of a household,
  that household's content is deleted with them. It belongs to nobody else at
  that point, and leaving it would mean the user's data was not deleted.

- No new permissions are requested, no new data is collected, and nothing new
  is sent anywhere. The app remains end-to-end encrypted; the new setting
  stores a permission value and a list of member identifiers, which are not
  content and are not encrypted, because the server has to be able to enforce
  them.

- The screenshots are unchanged from 1.9.0. The two screens this release adds
  live inside Settings and none of the ten screenshots in the listing shows a
  screen that has changed — verified by reading the text out of each one rather
  than assuming.
