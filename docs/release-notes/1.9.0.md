Photos that keep up, and a tidier way to leave.

• A photo now appears the moment it arrives. If someone else in the household adds one while you are looking at a task, it turns up on its own — no going back and in again — and the "arriving" label on your own photo clears itself as soon as the upload finishes.

• Deleting your account no longer removes anybody else. It takes your account, your data and your place in the household, and leaves everyone else's household exactly as it was.

• The code you use to connect an AI assistant now disappears from your clipboard when it expires, instead of sitting there afterwards.
