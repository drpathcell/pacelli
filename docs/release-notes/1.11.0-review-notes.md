No account is required — tap "Continue as guest" on the welcome screen and the
app is fully usable.

This release adds a detail view behind the picture on a shopping-list item.

To test:

1. Checklists tab -> open or create a checklist -> add an item -> swipe the row
   leading edge -> "Photo" -> attach a picture from the camera or library. A
   small thumbnail appears on the row.

2. Tap that thumbnail. A sheet opens with the picture at full width. "Done"
   closes it. For an item added by hand this is all the sheet contains.

3. When a household has connected an AI assistant (Settings -> Connect an AI,
   introduced in 1.7.0) and the assistant adds items from a supermarket's
   catalogue, the same sheet also shows the price at the time the item was
   added, the product's label information (ingredients, allergy advice,
   storage, origin) and a nutrition table. Reviewers will not see this part
   without such an assistant; a row without that information simply shows the
   picture, as in step 2.

Notes for review:

- No new permissions are requested and no new data is collected from the
  user. The product information in point 3 is public retailer catalogue text
  and imagery, fetched by our own server (never by the device) and stored on
  the item encrypted like every other field, so it reads offline in a shop.

- The app does not contact any retailer. Product pictures are copied once into
  our own storage bucket by the server and delivered to the device through the
  existing encrypted photo mechanism that shipped in 1.8.0.

- The screenshots are unchanged from 1.10.0. The only new screen is the
  detail sheet in point 2, which none of the listing screenshots shows.
