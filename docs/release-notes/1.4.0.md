Notifications from the other person.

When someone else in your household adds a task, your phone now tells you — and
it tells you what the task was, not just that something happened.

That last part is the interesting bit. Pacelli encrypts your tasks so that only
your household can read them, which normally means a notification can't say
anything useful. Here the task's name arrives still encrypted and is unlocked
on your own phone, a moment before it appears. Nobody in between — not us, not
Apple — can read it.

It's off by default. Turn it on in Settings, under Reminders.
