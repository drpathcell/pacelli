Shopping lists you can cost before you go.

• Long item names now wrap onto as many lines as they need instead of being cut off.

• Items with a shelf price show it under the name, with the line total when you buy more than one.

• A total at the foot of the list adds up every priced item, and once you start ticking things off it splits into what is in the trolley and what is still to get. Items without a price are left out and counted, so you know what the total covers.
