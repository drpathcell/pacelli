Pacelli can now lock itself.

• Face ID — turn on "Require Face ID" in Settings › Privacy and Pacelli locks whenever you switch away from it. Your data was always encrypted; this stops someone holding your unlocked phone from reading it.

• Checklist items can be edited — change an item's name or quantity in place, instead of deleting it and starting again.

• "Send feedback" has moved to its own Help section in Settings, so you can actually find it.
